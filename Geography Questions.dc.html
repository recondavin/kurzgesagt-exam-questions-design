A full-mark answer reads

Newton's Third Law: for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. The rower's legs, back and arms drive the blade so that it applies a backward force on the water. The water applies an equal force forward on the blade, which is transferred through the oar and the gate into the hull, so the boat accelerates towards the bow. A stronger drive means a bigger action force, so a bigger reaction and a faster boat.

Three boxes instead of one blank page: students lose marks on “explain” questions by describing the movement and never naming the force pair. Each box is one marking-scheme line, so a half-written answer still shows where the gap is.
